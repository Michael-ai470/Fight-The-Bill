Why this exists

The number on a medical bill is an opening position. Insurers pay a fraction of it. Hospitals have written policies describing exactly when they will reduce it, and many of those policies are legally required to be public.

A denied claim is a first response, not a verdict. You have appeal rights with deadlines that bind your insurer, and a right to review by someone who does not work for them.

Almost nobody uses any of this. That is the entire reason it keeps working.
